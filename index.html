Open in your browser

This app's built-in browser can't turn sideways, and SumbaSurf is played with your phone on its side.

  1. Tap the three dots at the top right of this screen.
  2. Choose Open in external browser.
  3. Turn your phone sideways and play.

SumbaSurf plays on its side, one thumb on each half of the screen, like holding a controller.
